# Ready for Limited Customer Pilot

---

## Why this option (and not the others)

### Not “Ready for Customer Pilot” (unqualified)
Unqualified customer pilot implies broader, more self-sufficient rollout. IAM V1.5 still carries interim scoring disclosure, soft identity, thin outcome-comparability UI, and console-grade aesthetics. Those do not block a **limited** pilot; they do block claiming general customer-pilot readiness without caveats.

### Not “Requires Additional Refinement Before Pilot”
EEP resolved the original walkthrough’s high-priority packaging failures (hierarchy, rationale, prompts, capacity, WIP, meeting flow). Holding all pilots for remaining polish would under-use a product that is now strong enough to learn from real Mondays. Remaining work belongs in pilot playbooks and V1.6 — not another pre-pilot freeze.

### Why “Limited Customer Pilot” is correct
- Original CX issues: mostly **Fully** or **Partially** resolved; critical packaging gaps closed.
- Scorecard overall **~84** (limited-pilot band).
- Commercial stance: **paid POV / limited pilot yes; immediate full purchase no.**
- Blueprint + EEP intent achieved: calm recurring management tool shape without ADR/scoring/lifecycle redesign.

## Remaining opportunities (categorized)

### Critical Issues
**For limited customer pilot: none that must block start**, assuming a pilot playbook covers Present→Approve, owner id conventions, and interim scoring messaging.

**If “customer pilot” is interpreted as broad / unattended / production:** the following become Critical (they are **out of EEP scope**, not EEP failures):
1. Authentication / RBAC (Phase 6)
2. Defensible final or bounded scoring narrative (SD-1 packaging)
3. Stronger outcome validation / expected-vs-actual presentation

*Independent validation does not reclassify those as EEP defects.*

### Version 1.6 Recommendations
1. Qualitative “if we wait” when COI is null (no invented formulas)
2. Explicit pairwise ranking rationale (“why this over the next Waiting item”)
3. People directory / assignee picker
4. Present+Approve simplification for Active Priorities (without breaking governance)
5. Board-native Complete / Outcome Validation “close the loop” checklist
6. Richer Recently Completed expected-vs-actual
7. Side-panel drill-down / meeting mode (large Active Priorities only)
8. Trust Pyramid content enrichment from deeper evidence narratives
9. Commercial visual system pass (without changing decision model)
10. Accessibility beyond baseline

### Nice-to-Have Improvements
1. Side-by-side Active Priority comparison
2. Printable / projector agenda
3. OEC change requiring dual approval / stronger audit UX
4. Owner mobile-lite progress update
5. Demo dataset + script packaged for sales
6. Collapse-state remembered per persona
